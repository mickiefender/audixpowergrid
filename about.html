About Audix Powergrid

Excellence in Electrical Engineering Since 2020

Audix Powergrid Engineering Limited has been at the forefront of electrical engineering solutions in Ghana for over two decades. We specialize in providing comprehensive electrical services for residential, commercial, and industrial clients.

Our commitment to excellence, safety, and customer satisfaction has made us a trusted name in the electrical industry. We combine traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver solutions that exceed expectations.

From simple home wiring to complex industrial installations, our team of certified electricians brings expertise, professionalism, and reliability to every project we undertake.
